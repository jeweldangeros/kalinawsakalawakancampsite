Welcome to Serenity

Escape to Doña Remedios Trinidad

Kalinaw sa Kalawakan is a peaceful, rustic campsite nestled in the lush landscapes of DRT, Bulacan. We are not a luxury glamping resort, but a sanctuary of natural simplicity.

Here, you'll fall asleep under star-filled skies, wake to the beautiful sound of chirping birds, and feel the cold breeze. Take a traditional Kawa bath, gather around a crackling bonfire, or splash in our refreshing public pool. It's the perfect environment to switch off your screens (cellular signals are Globe/TM only!) and reconnect with your loved ones, yourself, and nature.
